Refund Policy

Sandham Adams Inc. — refunds and cancellations

Effective date: August 31, 2026  |  Last updated: August 31, 2026
Please note: this Refund Policy covers refunds from Sandham Adams Inc. only. It does not cover chargebacks or refunds of card transactions processed through the Energy Payments platform — those are between the merchant and the cardholder, and are handled through the merchant's payment processor and the applicable card brand rules.

1. Energy Payments Platform

The Energy Payments extension is currently distributed through Microsoft AppSource. If Microsoft charges a subscription fee or license fee for the extension, refunds for those fees are governed by Microsoft's AppSource refund policy and must be requested directly from Microsoft.

Where Sandham Adams Inc. bills a merchant directly for the platform (for example, a private plan or add-on service), the following applies:

  • Trial or evaluation period: if a trial period is offered, no charge applies during the trial.
  • Annual subscriptions: refundable on a pro-rata basis within the first 30 days of the annual term, less any transaction volume or usage already consumed. After 30 days, subscriptions are non-refundable for the remainder of the term.
  • Monthly subscriptions: may be cancelled at any time; no refunds of the current month's fee.
  • Custom implementation or setup fees: non-refundable once work has begun.

2. Microsoft Business Central Licensing

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central licenses sold through Sandham Adams Inc. are governed by Microsoft's Cloud Solution Provider (CSP) refund and cancellation terms:

  • Monthly commitment: may be cancelled with a full refund within 7 calendar days of purchase.
  • Annual commitment: may be cancelled with a pro-rata refund within 7 calendar days of purchase. After 7 days, annual commitments are non-refundable for the remainder of the term.
  • Triennial commitment: same 7-day cancellation window as annual; non-refundable thereafter.

These terms may be updated by Microsoft; the version in effect at the time of purchase applies.

3. Consulting Services

Consulting, implementation, customization, training, and support services are billed against a signed Statement of Work (SOW) between the client and Sandham Adams Inc. Refunds and cancellations for those services are governed by the applicable SOW. In the absence of specific terms in the SOW:

  • Time-and-materials engagements: billable hours worked are non-refundable. Future work may be cancelled with reasonable notice.
  • Fixed-fee engagements: refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis based on the proportion of work completed and delivered.
  • Prepaid support blocks: unused hours may be refunded within 30 days of purchase; after 30 days, unused hours remain available for the term specified.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund covered by this Policy, contact us with:

  • The name and email under which the purchase was made;
  • The invoice or order number;
  • The reason for the refund request.

We aim to respond within five (5) business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within ten (10) business days.

5. Card Transactions Processed Through Energy Payments

Sandham Adams Inc. is not a party to payment transactions processed by merchants using the Energy Payments extension. Refund requests for those transactions must be directed to the merchant that charged your card. If the merchant does not resolve your request, you may:

  • Contact your card issuer to initiate a chargeback;
  • Contact the merchant's payment processor if you know who it is (this information typically appears on your card statement).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any revision. Refunds are governed by the version of this Policy in effect at the time of purchase.
