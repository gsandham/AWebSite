Privacy Policy

How Sandham Adams Inc. handles your personal information

Effective date: August 31, 2026  |  Last updated: August 31, 2026

Sandham Adams Inc. ("Sandham Adams", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where applicable, Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector ("Law 25").

Cardholder data notice: The Energy Payments platform uses hosted tokenization, meaning cardholder data (card numbers, CVVs) is captured directly by the underlying payment processor's secure hosted iframe and never touches Sandham Adams servers, Microsoft Business Central tenants, or any system operated by Sandham Adams. We do not store, transmit, or have access to cardholder data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 On sandhamadams.com

  • Contact form submissions: your name, email, company name, phone number (optional), and message content.
  • Business Central license order requests: in addition to the above, the edition, billing term, and number of users.
  • Rate-limiting data: a locally-stored timestamp in your browser to prevent form-submission abuse. This stays on your device.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider retains standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, page requested, timestamp) for security and operational purposes.

1.2 On the Energy Payments platform

  • Merchant configuration: API credentials for the underlying payment processor, stored in the merchant's own Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central tenant. Sandham Adams does not have access to any merchant's tenant data.
  • Customer identifiers: customer numbers and names (from your Business Central customer records) may be transmitted to the underlying payment processor when initiating a transaction, so the transaction can be tied to a customer for reconciliation. No sensitive personal data or cardholder data is transmitted by us.
  • Payment tokens: non-sensitive tokens issued by the underlying payment processor (masked card numbers, expiry, card brand) are stored in the merchant's Business Central tenant.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries or license requests submitted through the Site;
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Site and the Energy Payments platform;
  • Communicate with you about your engagement with Sandham Adams;
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: our hosting provider (which stores the Site's server logs) and Microsoft (as the platform provider for Business Central and AppSource) may process information incidental to providing their services to us.
  • Legal obligations: if required to do so by law, court order, or a lawful request from a regulatory authority.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the same protections as this Policy.

4. Data Retention

We keep contact-form and inquiry information only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, complete any resulting engagement, and satisfy our legal and tax obligations (typically up to 7 years for financial records).

5. Where Your Information Is Stored

Personal information collected through the Site is stored on servers located in Canada. Information you enter into the Energy Payments platform is stored in your own Microsoft Business Central tenant, which Microsoft hosts in the region you selected when provisioning your tenant.

6. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA, and where applicable Quebec Law 25, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Withdraw your consent to our processing of your information (subject to legal and contractual restrictions);
  • Request that we cease processing or delete your information;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, if you are in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal information we hold from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The Site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The Site uses localStorage in your browser only for rate-limiting form submissions. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the Site.

9. Children

The Site and Platform are not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any revisions. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
